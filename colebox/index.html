The collaboration

Two teams. One simpler direction.

ColeBox brings the fintech product. Hoberg brings digital product, design and technology expertise. Together, the goal is to create a smoother way for people to interact with financial services through a channel they already use every day.

01 / COLEBOX

Fintech, built around WhatsApp

ColeBox is building a fintech experience that lets users carry out transactions through WhatsApp, reducing friction between intent and action.

02 / HOBERG

Digital product & technology

Hoberg Digital Agency works across digital experiences, web technology and product execution to turn ideas into useful, polished products.

03 / TOGETHER

A more familiar experience

The collaboration brings product and digital expertise together around one idea: make financial interactions feel less complicated.

What we're building

Transactions, without the extra friction.

The experience is being designed around WhatsApp so users can interact with the service in a familiar environment instead of learning an entirely new workflow.
