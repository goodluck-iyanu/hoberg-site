LAGOS, NIGERIA — Hoberg Digital operates as a unified Lagos technology and services group founded and owned by Goodluck Iyanu. From the group map at hoberg.com.ng to our dedicated agency, employment, scholarship, utility, and product divisions, every property shares a commitment to clarity, utility, and verifiable execution for Nigerians.

The Hoberg Visual Identity

The Hoberg Digital Agency mark combines a confident red-and-white symbol with a refined gold wordmark on deep obsidian black, paired with warm editorial paper tones across our group properties. The visual system was designed to feel bold, professional, and memorable — reflecting an organisation built to help Nigerian businesses stand out online.

Group Doors & Official Properties

Hoberg Digital organises its activities into clear, dedicated doors so clients, job seekers, students, and partners can go straight to what they need:

  • Hoberg Digital Agency & Services: Websites, mobile apps, e-commerce, SEO, AI search visibility, content, ads, and custom software systems for Nigerian businesses across four service pillars — Digital Presence, Digital Visibility, Digital Growth, and Digital Systems.
  • Hoberg Jobs: Free job search and hiring platform for Nigerians. Job seekers never pay to apply.
  • Hoberg Edu: Discovery platform for verified study-abroad programmes and scholarships (admissions are decided by the official institutions).
  • Hoberg Tools: Free browser utilities built in Lagos, including TikDrop, FileDrop, IMGDrop, and BGDrop.
  • Hoberg Research: Original market notes, technical audits, and data reports from the Hoberg desk.
  • Ventures & Products: Colebox, Maji by Hoberg, and Healthnehome.
  • FICQS: A facility services sister company in the Hoberg group, operated separately from the digital products.
