Hoberg Digital Launches "#SellOnMaji" Campaign to Empower Nigerian Merchants with Zero-Commission Storefronts⁠

Anchored by the message "Nothing Can Slow You Down When You Sell on Maji," the multi-vendor marketplace eliminates seller commission cuts so independent vendors keep 100% of their listed product price.⁠

LAGOS, NIGERIA — Hoberg Digital Agency today announced the official rollout of the #SellOnMaji merchant growth campaign for Maji (maji.hoberg.com.ng), its multi-vendor e-commerce marketplace engineered for Nigerian small businesses, independent vendors, and social commerce entrepreneurs. Centered around the campaign theme, "Nothing Can Slow You Down When You Sell on Maji — Keep Selling. Go Further," the initiative invites merchants across Nigeria to transition from fragmented social media direct messages into dedicated, automated online storefronts.

Lede Box: Your Storefront. Everyone's Marketplace. Maji equips Nigerian merchants with an instant digital storefront, Paystack-powered escrow-backed checkout, and a transparent fee model where sellers keep 100% of their listed product price.

Eliminating the Bottlenecks of Social Commerce

Across Nigeria, thousands of high-potential vendors lose daily revenue to "DM for price" friction, manual bank transfer verifications, ghost buyers, and steep commission deductions on legacy marketplaces. Maji removes these operational roadblocks by giving every vendor a dedicated storefront inside a unified discovery marketplace.

Whether operating from a retail shop, a campus hostel, or a living room couch with a smartphone, merchants on Maji can list inventory in minutes, share direct product links across WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, and X, and receive instant order notifications without chasing screenshots.

Key Details & Platform Highlights

  • ✓ 100% Seller Margin Retention: Unlike platforms that deduct 10% to 20% from vendor payouts, Maji charges a transparent 4% + ₦50 platform fee paid by the buyer at checkout, ensuring merchants retain 100% of their listed product price.
  • ✓ Instant Storefront Architecture: Vendors get a clean, mobile-first storefront under the Maji ecosystem (maji.hoberg.com.ng) designed for fast loading and frictionless guest or account checkout.
  • ✓ Verified Payment & Order Tracking: Integrated with Paystack and structured order IDs (ORD-*), protecting both buyers and sellers from payment anxiety.
  • ✓ Built for Mobile-First Hustlers: Designed so business owners can manage inventory, track payouts, and fulfill orders entirely from their mobile devices.

Frequently Asked Questions

Maji is a Nigerian multi-vendor e-commerce marketplace developed by Hoberg Digital Agency that enables small businesses and independent sellers to create instant online storefronts, accept secure payments, and reach customers nationwide under the banner "Your Storefront. Everyone's Marketplace.

Creating a seller storefront on Maji is free, and sellers keep 100% of their listed product price. A transparent platform fee of 4% + ₦50 is paid by the buyer at checkout rather than deducted from the merchant's product earnings.

Visit [https://maji.hoberg.com.ng](https://maji.hoberg.com.ng), register your seller account, upload your product catalog with clear pricing and photos, and share your storefront link on social media using the hashtag #sellonmaji and tagging @maji.hoberg.com.ng.

All transactions on Maji are processed securely via Paystack with unique order tracking IDs, ensuring verified payment confirmation for merchants and purchase confidence for buyers.
