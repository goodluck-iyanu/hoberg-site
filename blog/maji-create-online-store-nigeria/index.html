What Is Maji?

Maji is a Nigerian ecommerce platform developed by Hoberg Digital. It helps businesses create an online storefront where customers can discover products and shop online.

For businesses that currently depend on social media posts and private messages to sell products, having a dedicated storefront can provide a more organised way to display products, share a store link, and manage the customer shopping experience.

Maji aims to make it easier for businesses to establish their own online selling presence.

Create an Online Store in Nigeria with Maji

Starting an online store involves more than uploading product pictures. Businesses need a place to showcase their products, communicate prices, receive orders, and guide customers through the buying process.

Maji provides businesses with a platform to establish an online storefront without having to build an entire ecommerce website from scratch.

Businesses exploring how to create an online store in Nigeria can visit Maji to learn about its available features and determine whether the platform fits their needs.

An Online Store for Businesses Beyond Social Media

WhatsApp, Instagram, and other social platforms can help businesses attract customers. However, managing product enquiries, prices, and orders entirely through conversations can become difficult as a business grows.

A dedicated online storefront gives customers a central place to browse products and gives sellers a link they can share across their social channels, marketing campaigns, and other customer touchpoints.

Maji is designed to support businesses looking for a more structured way to present and sell their products online.

Supporting Physical and Digital Products

Businesses sell different types of products, from fashion and beauty items to downloadable products and other digital offerings.

Maji supports storefronts for physical and digital products, helping businesses present their offerings through an online selling platform.

Businesses should review the platform’s current features, payment arrangements, and any product-specific requirements before getting started.

What Should You Look for in an Ecommerce Platform?

Before choosing an online store builder, business owners should consider:

  • Ease of use: How easily can they create and maintain their storefront?
  • Product management: Can they organise and display their products clearly?
  • Payments: What payment methods and fees apply?
  • Delivery: What delivery options are available for physical products?
  • Store identity: Can they share their own storefront link, and what customisation options are available?
  • Costs: What features are free, and which require payment?

These considerations help businesses choose a platform that suits their needs and budget.

Why Maji?

Maji is part of Hoberg Digital’s broader effort to develop digital products for businesses and users in Nigeria.

Its focus is on helping businesses establish online storefronts and make their products easier to discover through a dedicated online presence.

For sellers looking for an alternative to managing every product enquiry through private messages, Maji offers an option worth exploring.
