LAGOS, NIGERIA — Hoberg Digital has published the 2026 Nigerian Business AI Visibility Report, an empirical audit of 50 prominent Nigerian enterprises, banks, telecoms, consumer brands, and technology platforms to evaluate how effectively modern search engines and AI answer systems can crawl, verify, and cite their official websites.

Executive Summary

As decision-makers increasingly use AI-assisted search tools — including ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Microsoft Copilot — alongside traditional Google and Bing search, a company's website must do more than render visual pages. It must provide machine-readable entity definitions, accurate metadata, canonical URLs, and verifiable structured data.

The Hoberg Digital Research Desk audited 50 Nigerian businesses across banking, industrial manufacturing, fintech, energy, logistics, and e-commerce — including Dangote, BUA Group, GTBank, Zenith Bank, Flutterwave, Paystack, Jumia, and Interswitch — to benchmark how Nigerian corporate web properties perform across core discoverability signals.

What the Audit Measured

Each of the 50 websites in the dataset was evaluated against verifiable technical criteria:

  • Document Title & Meta Description Clarity: Whether pages declare specific, non-duplicate titles and summaries that accurately describe the organisation.
  • Schema.org JSON-LD Entity Markup: Presence and validity of Organization, WebSite, Article, and FAQPage structured data.
  • Crawler & Bot Accessibility: Inspection of robots.txt, XML sitemaps, canonical tags, and HTTP status codes for search and AI retrieval crawlers.
  • Content Freshness & Newsroom Structure: Availability of an active, crawlable company blog, newsroom, or official press archive with clear publication dates.
  • Mobile Viewport & Core Rendering: Whether critical company facts are present in initial server/static HTML rather than hidden behind unindexed client scripts.

Frequently Asked Questions

AI visibility refers to how accurately AI-powered search and answer systems — such as ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Microsoft Copilot — can crawl, verify, and cite your official website when users ask questions about your industry or company.

Both the PDF report (Hoberg_Report_2026.pdf) and the Excel dataset (Hoberg_Master_50_Sites.xlsx) are available for direct download on the report page and in the Press Kit links on this release.

No configuration can force or guarantee citations by any search engine or AI model. However, clean crawlable HTML, accurate Schema.org JSON-LD, consistent entity attribution, and updated sitemaps remove technical barriers to legitimate discovery.
