LAGOS, NIGERIA — Hoberg Digital, the Lagos-based technology and digital product company founded and owned by Goodluck Iyanu, today announced the official launch of the Hoberg Digital Newsroom and Product Showroom at hoberg.com.ng/blog.

An Official Source of Record for Hoberg Digital

Built directly inside the main Hoberg Digital platform, the new newsroom serves as the permanent public archive and digital showroom for Hoberg's products, agency capabilities, research publications, partnerships, and corporate milestones.

Every announcement published in the Hoberg Digital Newsroom is issued at a permanent canonical URL under https://hoberg.com.ng/blog/[slug] with structured Schema.org metadata, verified publication timestamps, downloadable press resources, and syndication feeds (`RSS 2.0`, `Atom 1.0`, `JSON Feed`, and `llms.txt`).

What the Hoberg Newsroom & Showroom Covers

Visitors, clients, journalists, and researchers can browse and filter official releases across Hoberg Digital's core verticals:

  • Official Press Releases & Company Statements: Verified corporate updates, leadership communications, and business milestones from Hoberg Digital in Lagos.
  • Product Launches & Platform Showroom: Releases and feature updates across Hoberg Tools (TikDrop, FileDrop, IMGDrop, BGDrop), Hoberg Jobs, Hoberg Edu, Colebox, Maji by Hoberg, Healthnehome, and sister company FICQS.
  • Original Research & Benchmark Reports: Data-backed studies including the 2026 Nigerian Business AI Visibility Report auditing 50 leading Nigerian companies.
  • Web Engineering & Digital Visibility Guides: Practical technical guides from Hoberg Digital Agency and Hoberg Digital Visibility Services covering website architecture, e-commerce engineering, and search readiness in Nigeria.

Engineered for Open-Web Syndication and Technical Clarity

To ensure that every announcement is immediately accessible to readers, journalists, search engines, and AI research assistants, the Hoberg Digital Newsroom automatically maintains:

  • XML Sitemaps: hoberg.com.ng/sitemap.xml and hoberg.com.ng/blog/sitemap.xml with image and video metadata extensions.
  • Syndication Feeds: RSS 2.0 Feed, Atom 1.0 Feed, and JSON Feed 1.1.
  • AI & Entity Factsheets: hoberg.com.ng/llms.txt and hoberg.com.ng/blog/llms.txt for transparent machine-readable indexing.
Explore the Hoberg Ecosystem: Visit the Hoberg Digital Newsroom to read all current releases, or explore Hoberg Digital Agency to partner with our engineering and visibility team in Lagos.
