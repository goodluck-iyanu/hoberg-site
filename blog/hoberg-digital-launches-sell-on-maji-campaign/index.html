Hoberg Digital Launches #SellOnMaji Campaign to Help Nigerian Merchants Take Their Businesses Further

Hoberg Digital Agency unveils a new merchant growth campaign for Maji, its digital commerce platform, encouraging Nigerian entrepreneurs already selling through WhatsApp, Instagram and other channels to build, manage and grow their businesses with dedicated online storefronts and a connected marketplace.

LAGOS, NIGERIA — Hoberg Digital today announced the launch of #SellOnMaji, a merchant growth campaign designed to empower Nigerian entrepreneurs, small businesses and independent sellers to take their businesses further through Maji, its digital commerce platform.

Built around the campaign message, “You’re Already Selling. Now, Go Further.”, the initiative recognizes the thousands of Nigerian merchants already building businesses through WhatsApp, Instagram, social media and other informal sales channels.

While these platforms have made it easier to start selling, merchants often need more structured tools to manage their businesses, present their products professionally and provide customers with a convenient purchasing experience.

Maji aims to bridge that gap.

Introducing Maji: More Than Just an Online Store

Available at https://maji.hoberg.com.ng, Maji is a digital commerce platform that brings merchants and customers together through an accessible online shopping experience.

The platform enables merchants to establish dedicated digital storefronts, publish product listings, receive customer orders and manage their online business presence.

Maji also provides a marketplace environment where customers can discover products from different sellers and shop through a connected platform.

With support for physical and digital products, Maji is designed to accommodate different types of businesses and evolving merchant needs.

What Merchants Can Do on Maji

Through Maji, merchants gain access to tools and capabilities designed to make online selling more structured.

Key platform capabilities include:

  • Dedicated Online Storefronts: Create a digital home for your business where customers can browse your products.
  • Product Management: Add, organize and showcase products with descriptions, images and pricing.
  • Online Checkout: Give customers a structured purchasing experience with integrated payment processing.
  • Multi-Vendor Marketplace: Make products discoverable within a shared marketplace alongside other merchants.
  • Delivery Options: Support delivery arrangements for physical products, including available delivery integrations and arrangements with sellers.
  • Digital Products: Sell eligible digital products alongside physical merchandise.
  • Custom Domain Support: Give your storefront a more personalized business identity with a custom domain.
  • Mobile-Friendly Shopping: Make your storefront accessible to customers across mobile devices.

Maji is built to help merchants establish a stronger online presence without having to build an e-commerce platform from scratch.

The #SellOnMaji Campaign: You’re Already Selling. Now, Go Further.

The #SellOnMaji campaign is built on a simple idea: Nigerian entrepreneurs do not necessarily need to start their businesses all over again. They need better opportunities and tools to grow what they have already started.

Rather than asking merchants to abandon their existing audiences, Maji encourages them to build on their current selling activities by creating a dedicated storefront that customers can visit, browse and shop from.

The campaign will focus on merchant awareness, seller onboarding, storefront creation and product listing.

Hoberg Digital intends to engage existing merchants and introduce more Nigerian businesses to the opportunities available through Maji.

The initial campaign will prioritize helping merchants complete their storefront setup and publish their first products.

Supporting Nigerian Businesses Through Digital Commerce

Small businesses play an important role in Nigeria’s economy. However, many merchants still operate without dedicated websites, structured online catalogues or convenient digital purchasing experiences.

Maji is part of Hoberg Digital’s broader effort to build digital products that address practical business challenges and create more opportunities for individuals and businesses.

By bringing storefronts, product discovery, checkout and marketplace functionality into one environment, Maji seeks to make digital commerce more accessible to merchants at different stages of business growth.

About Hoberg Digital

Hoberg Digital is a Nigerian technology and digital business company focused on building digital products, platforms and solutions.

Through its products and initiatives, the company works towards making technology more accessible, helping businesses operate more effectively and creating digital opportunities across different sectors.

Hoberg Digital Agency operates as a component of Hoberg Digital, providing digital services and supporting the company’s broader ecosystem.

About Maji

Maji is a digital commerce platform developed by Hoberg Digital to help merchants establish online storefronts, list products and connect with customers through a shared marketplace.

The platform supports physical and digital product listings, online checkout and different selling and delivery arrangements.
