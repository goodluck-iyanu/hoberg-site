LAGOS, NIGERIA — Hoberg Digital continues to expand its consumer and creator utility ecosystem at hoberg.com.ng/tools, offering clean, fast browser tools built in Lagos — led by TikDrop alongside FileDrop, IMGDrop, and BGDrop.

Why Hoberg Builds Public Web Utilities

Alongside client engineering at Hoberg Digital Agency, Hoberg Digital builds and operates its own standalone software products. Shipping real utilities used directly in the browser keeps our engineering standards grounded in speed, mobile usability, and low-friction interfaces.

The Hoberg Tools Lineup

  • TikDrop: A straightforward TikTok video downloader web utility made by Hoberg for saving public TikTok videos through a clean browser interface.
  • FileDrop: Lightweight file transfer and sharing utility inside the Hoberg Tools ecosystem.
  • IMGDrop: Fast image conversion and optimisation tool for web and social workflows.
  • BGDrop: Quick background removal utility for product photos and marketing creatives.

Availability

All utilities are accessible through the central Hoberg Tools hub on mobile and desktop browsers.
