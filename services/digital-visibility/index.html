FAQ

Questions businesses ask.

What is Digital Visibility?

Digital visibility is how easily people can discover and understand your business across search engines, Google, online platforms and modern AI-powered search experiences.

Is Digital Visibility just SEO?

No. SEO is one part of it. Hoberg's Digital Visibility service also focuses on Google presence, AI-search readiness and reputation.

Can you help my business appear on Google?

We can improve your website's search foundation and help optimize your Google Business presence where appropriate. Search rankings are not guaranteed because search engines determine their own results.

Can you guarantee a #1 Google ranking?

No. Nobody should honestly guarantee a specific Google ranking. We focus on sustainable improvements to your website, content, technical SEO and overall visibility.

What is AI Search visibility?

It means making your business information clear, consistent, useful and structured so modern search systems have better information with which to understand your business.

How much does Digital Visibility cost?

Hoberg offers a free initial visibility audit. Ongoing SEO and visibility work starts from the published starting price, while larger requirements are quoted based on scope.
