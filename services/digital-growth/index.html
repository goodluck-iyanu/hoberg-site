FAQ

Questions businesses ask.

What is Digital Growth?

Digital Growth is the use of digital channels such as content, social media, advertising and lead generation to create awareness, demand and business opportunities.

Do you only manage social media?

No. Social media is only one part of the service. Hoberg can combine content, social media, advertising and lead generation depending on what your business actually needs.

Does the price include advertising money?

No. Advertising spend is separate from Hoberg's service fee. Your advertising budget can be planned based on your goals and available resources.

Can you guarantee leads or sales?

No legitimate agency can guarantee a specific number of sales or leads in every situation. We focus on building, testing and improving the systems that can generate better opportunities.

Which platforms can you work with?

Depending on the campaign, social and advertising strategies can be developed for platforms such as Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Google and other relevant channels.

Can you build a complete growth campaign?

Yes. Hoberg can combine content, social media, advertising and lead-generation activities into a single campaign based on your business objectives.
