FAQ

Questions businesses ask.

What are Digital Systems?

Digital Systems are software, automation, artificial intelligence and integrations that help a business operate, communicate and process information more efficiently.

Can Hoberg build custom software?

Yes. Hoberg can develop custom web applications, mobile applications, dashboards and internal business software based on defined requirements.

Can you automate my existing business process?

Yes. The first step is understanding the current workflow and identifying repetitive or manual processes that could potentially be automated.

Can AI be added to an existing business system?

In many cases, yes. AI can potentially be connected to existing software, workflows or customer experiences depending on the technical architecture.

Can you connect different platforms?

Yes. Systems can be connected through APIs and other integration methods where the platforms provide suitable technical access.

How much does custom software cost?

The cost depends on the features, users, integrations, infrastructure, security requirements and overall complexity. Custom projects are normally quoted after the requirements are understood.
